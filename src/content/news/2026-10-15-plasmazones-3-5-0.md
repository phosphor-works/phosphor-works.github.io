- Six blur packs gain brightness, contrast, saturation, vibrancy and noise controls
- Glass refraction gains the controls Better Blur's refraction has
- Blur presets for the looks people compare against
- Every pack that draws a pane's outline can round or square its bottom corners, and most can soften that edge
- A window on all desktops keeps its own place on each one
- Dropping a window on a zone of another layout in the zone selector moves the other windows too
